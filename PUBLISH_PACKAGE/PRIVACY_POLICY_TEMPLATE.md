# Privacy Policy for Athkari Islamic App

**Last updated: December 2024**

## Information We Collect
This Islamic app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

## Data Usage
- The app works offline
- No user data is transmitted to external servers
- No personal information is required to use the app

## Islamic Content
- All content is sourced from authentic Islamic texts
- Quran verses and Islamic prayers are provided for religious purposes
- No modifications are made to religious texts

## Permissions
The app may request the following permissions:
- **Location**: For Qibla direction and prayer times (optional)
- **Notifications**: For prayer time reminders (optional)
- **Storage**: For offline content and user preferences

## Third-Party Services
This app does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Children's Privacy
This app is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy. Users will be notified of any changes.

## Contact Us
For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the app developer.

*This app is created to serve the Muslim community and follows Islamic principles of privacy and respect.*
